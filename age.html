Age Calculator — How to Calculate Your Exact Age Online

FinanceKit Pro's free age calculator determines your exact age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes from your date of birth to today — or any specific date you choose. Unlike simple year subtraction, our calculator accounts for leap years, varying month lengths (28–31 days), and even your birth time for sub-minute precision. Trusted by users in 150+ countries including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Germany, and Singapore.

How Does the Age Calculator Work?

The calculator uses your birth date and today's date to compute a chronological age. The algorithm works as follows:

  • Years: Subtract birth year from current year. Subtract 1 if the birthday hasn't occurred yet this year.
  • Months: Calculate remaining months after full years, accounting for whether the day of month has passed.
  • Days: Calculate remaining days after full months, using the actual number of days in each month.
  • Total Days: Multiply years by 365.25 (accounts for leap years) and add remaining months × 30.44 and remaining days.
  • Hours & Minutes: Multiply total days by 24 (hours) and 1,440 (minutes).

Age Calculation Formula

The standard age formula used internationally is: Age = Current Date − Date of Birth. In practice:

  • If today is April 30, 2026 and you were born on March 15, 1990: Age = 36 years, 1 month, 15 days
  • Total days = (36 × 365.25) + (1 × 30.44) + 15 ≈ 13,200 days
  • Total hours = 13,200 × 24 = 316,800+ hours

Leap Year Handling

A leap year occurs every 4 years (divisible by 4), except century years (divisible by 100) unless also divisible by 400. For people born on February 29:

  • In leap years: Birthday is celebrated on February 29
  • In non-leap years: Birthday is celebrated on February 28 in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, and most European countries
  • In non-leap years: Birthday is celebrated on March 1 in Hong Kong (per court ruling)
