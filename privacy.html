1. Overview & Scope of This Privacy Policy

Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how FinanceKit Pro ("we," "us," "our"), accessible at financekit.tech, handles information when you use our suite of free financial calculators, health tools, live market data pages, economic calendar, and crypto tracking tools.

FinanceKit Pro is a browser-based, zero-account platform. We have designed every tool — from our free EMI calculator and compound interest calculator to our live Bitcoin price tracker and economic calendar for forex — to operate entirely within your web browser without requiring any personal information from you.

📌 Short version: We don't know who you are, where you live, what loans you're calculating, or what crypto you track. All tool inputs stay in your browser. We collect only anonymized, aggregated usage analytics through Google Analytics. We will never sell your data.

This policy applies to all visitors worldwide, including users in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland, Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, and all other jurisdictions. If you are accessing our tools from the European Union or United Kingdom, additional rights apply to you under GDPR, which are described in the Compliance section below.

7. Google Analytics — Anonymized Usage Data

We only see aggregate trends, never individuals

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how people use FinanceKit Pro in aggregate. This helps us identify which tools are most useful, which pages load slowly, and where we can improve. Our GA4 setup is configured with:

  • IP anonymization enabled — your full IP address is never stored; only a truncated, non-identifiable version is used to derive approximate country/region
  • Data retention set to 14 months — analytics data is automatically deleted after this period
  • Google Signals disabled — we do not allow cross-device tracking or linking to Google account profiles
  • Advertising features disabled — we do not use GA4 for remarketing audience creation
  • No user-ID tracking — since we have no accounts, there are no user IDs to track

In practice, we see reports like "FinanceKit Pro received 45,000 visits this month; 22% from the US, 8% from the UK, 6% from Germany." We never see individual user journeys, personally identifiable information, or financial data from any calculator.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking on any website, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

9. Your Privacy Rights & How to Exercise Them

Access, deletion, portability, and opt-out

Depending on your country of residence, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your data. Because FinanceKit Pro does not collect personal data, exercising most of these rights results in a response of "no data held" — but we are committed to honoring every request transparently.

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request deletion of your personal data. Since we hold none, we will confirm this in writing.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate personal data we hold.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to Object / Opt-Out: You can opt out of analytics tracking via the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or from ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we restrict how we use your data while a complaint is being investigated.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If you have provided consent for any processing, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@financekit.tech with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We aim to respond within 2 business days for simple requests and within 30 days for complex data subject access requests.

If you are in the EU or UK and believe we have not handled your data appropriately, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA). In the UK, this is the ICO (ico.org.uk). In Germany, it is the relevant Landesbeauftragter. We strongly encourage you to contact us first so we can resolve any concerns directly.

11. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

How we keep your data safe — even the minimal data we handle

Our best security measure is architectural: since we collect no personal data, there is no database of user records to breach, no password hashes to steal, and no financial information to expose. However, we also implement standard web security practices:

  • HTTPS everywhere: All pages are served over TLS/SSL encryption. HTTP connections are automatically redirected to HTTPS.
  • No user database: There is no login system, no user accounts, and no server-side user profile storage.
  • No form submissions to our servers: Calculator inputs are processed entirely in your browser's JavaScript engine and never transmitted anywhere.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers: Implemented to reduce XSS attack vectors.
  • Minimal third-party scripts: We only load scripts from Google (Analytics, AdSense, Fonts) and no other third parties, reducing attack surface.
  • Subresource Integrity (SRI): Planned for all external scripts to ensure tamper-proof delivery.

In the unlikely event of a security incident affecting any data we do hold (e.g., server-level access logs), we will notify affected parties and relevant authorities within 72 hours as required by GDPR Article 33.
