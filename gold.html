How Is the International Gold Price Determined?

Gold is priced globally in US Dollars (USD) per troy ounce on the COMEX (Chicago Mercantile Exchange) and the LBMA (London Bullion Market Association). The "spot price" is the current price for immediate delivery. The London Gold Fix, set twice daily at 10:30 AM and 3:00 PM GMT, is the globally recognized benchmark.

To convert to local currencies like PKR, INR, or EUR, the international spot price is multiplied by the live USD exchange rate. For example: if gold is at $3,200/oz and USD/PKR is 280, then gold = 3,200 × 280 = PKR 896,000 per troy ounce.

Gold Price Per Gram, Tola, Ounce & Kg — How to Convert

Different regions use different weight units for gold:

  • Troy Ounce (oz): The global standard. 1 troy oz = 31.1035 grams.
  • Gram (g): Widely used worldwide. Price/gram = Spot Price ÷ 31.1035.
  • Tola: Traditional unit in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, and the Middle East. 1 tola = 11.6638 grams. Widely used in Sarafa markets.
  • Kilogram (kg): Used for larger quantities. 1 kg = 1000g = 32.1507 troy oz.

Our calculator above handles all these conversions automatically. Simply enter your weight, select your unit and currency, and get the live value instantly.

Gold Rate in Pakistan (PKR) — Today's 24K, 22K, 18K Tola Rate

In Pakistan, gold is most commonly traded in tolas through Sarafa (gold market) dealers. The Pakistan Sarafa Association (Sarrafa Bazar) sets the daily gold rate in Pakistan. 24K gold (pure gold) is the benchmark, while 22K gold (916 hallmark) is used for most gold jewelry. The Sarafa market operates in major cities including Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad.

Our live gold rate in Pakistan PKR is calculated from the international spot price converted at the current USD/PKR exchange rate. Actual Sarafa market prices may vary slightly due to local making charges and dealer margins.

What Factors Affect Gold Prices in 2026?

Gold prices are driven by a complex mix of global factors:

  • US Dollar Strength: Gold and USD have an inverse relationship — a weaker dollar pushes gold higher.
  • Interest Rates: When central banks (especially the US Fed) raise rates, gold becomes less attractive vs yield-bearing assets.
  • Geopolitical Tensions: War, sanctions, political instability → investors flee to gold as a safe haven.
  • Inflation: Gold is historically an inflation hedge. Rising CPI often correlates with rising gold prices.
  • Central Bank Buying: Countries like China, India, and Russia have been buying gold reserves, boosting demand.
  • Gold ETF Flows: When ETFs like GLD and IAU see inflows, spot demand rises.

Is Gold a Good Investment in 2026?

Gold remains one of the most time-tested assets in any diversified portfolio. In 2026, with persistent inflation concerns and geopolitical uncertainty, financial advisors often recommend allocating 5–15% of your portfolio to gold. Ways to invest in gold include physical gold (bars, coins, jewelry), Gold ETFs (like GLD, IAU), Sovereign Gold Bonds (India), Gold Futures (COMEX), and Digital Gold platforms.

Note: This is general information only. Always consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K, and 18K gold? ▼
24 karat (24K) gold is 99.9% pure gold — used for investment bars, coins, and bullion. 22 karat (22K) gold is 91.67% pure and is the most popular for jewelry in South Asia and the Middle East due to its durability. 18 karat (18K) gold is 75% pure and popular in Western fine jewelry and watches. The price scales proportionally: 22K = 24K price × 0.9167; 18K = 24K price × 0.75.

Use the Gold Weight Calculator on this page. Enter your weight in grams (or tola/oz/kg), select the purity (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K), and choose your currency. The formula is: Value = (Weight in grams ÷ 31.1035) × Spot Price per troy oz × Purity Factor × Exchange Rate. Our calculator handles all of this automatically with live data. Note: this gives the melt value — jewelry retail prices are higher due to making charges.
How many grams are in one tola of gold? ▼
One tola is equal to exactly 11.6638 grams (sometimes rounded to 11.66g). The tola is a traditional unit of weight in South Asia and is officially recognized in Pakistan and India for gold trading. 1 troy ounce = 2.6675 tolas. 1 tola = approximately 0.375 troy ounces. Our calculator and rates table show prices in tola by default for PKR and INR views.
Why does the gold price change every day? ▼
Gold prices fluctuate daily due to: US dollar strength (inverse relationship), inflation data and CPI reports, Federal Reserve and central bank policy decisions, geopolitical events and crises, gold ETF inflows/outflows, mining supply data, and overall risk sentiment in financial markets. During economic uncertainty, gold typically rises as investors seek safe-haven assets. During risk-on market rallies, gold may fall as investors prefer equities.
What is the gold price per kg today in USD? ▼
The gold price per kilogram = Spot Price per troy oz × 32.1507 (troy oz per kg). For example, if gold is $3,200 per troy oz, then 1 kg of 24K gold = $3,200 × 32.1507 ≈ $102,882. Check the rates table above (set to USD) to see today's live gold price per kg for all purity levels. You can also use our calculator to enter "1 kg" and get the exact value.
Is it safe to buy gold online or from a dealer? ▼
When buying physical gold, always verify the hallmark (BIS in India, PMDA in Pakistan for jewelry, or assay certifications for bars/coins). Buy from licensed dealers and reputable mints (PAMP Suisse, Perth Mint, US Mint, etc.). For digital gold or gold ETFs, use SEBI-regulated platforms (India) or SEC-regulated brokers (US). Always check the making/fabrication charges when buying jewelry, as these are separate from the gold melt value.
