How Does the Live Crypto Converter Work?

Our tool uses two live API sources running in parallel:

  • CoinGecko API — Real-time prices for 200+ cryptocurrencies in USD, refreshed every 60 seconds from hundreds of global exchanges.
  • ExchangeRate API — Live fiat currency rates for USD to 170+ currencies, updated continuously.

The conversion formula is: Result = (Amount × Crypto USD Price) × Fiat Exchange Rate. For crypto-to-crypto conversions, both coins' USD prices are used as the bridge rate — exactly how major exchanges like Coinbase and Binance calculate pairs.

Tax Considerations for Crypto Conversion

In most countries — including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and Germany — converting cryptocurrency is a taxable event. The taxable gain is calculated as: Sale Price (in fiat) − Cost Basis (original purchase price in fiat). Our converter shows the current market value in your chosen currency, which you can use for tax documentation. Always consult a qualified tax professional for your specific situation.
