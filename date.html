Free Date Calculator Online 2026 — How to Use

FinanceKit Pro's Date Calculator is one of the most powerful free tools available online. Whether you need to calculate the exact number of days between two dates, find out what date falls 90 business days from now, count down to your next holiday, or simply use a full scientific calculator — everything is available in one place, completely free, with no account required.

How to Calculate Days Between Two Dates

Use the Date Difference tab. Enter any start date and end date — past, present, or future — and click "Calculate Difference." The tool instantly returns the number of days, weeks, months, business days, and weekends between those two dates. You can also choose whether to include the end date in the count.

Example: From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026 = 364 days total, approximately 261 business days, and 104 weekend days.

Adding or Subtracting Days from a Date

The Add / Subtract Days tab lets you enter any date and add or subtract any number of days, weeks, months, or years. You can also choose to skip weekends and calculate only in business days — ideal for project deadlines, payment due dates, contract terms, and court filing deadlines.

Example: If your contract starts March 15, 2026 and has a 90-day delivery window (business days only), the due date falls on approximately July 22, 2026.

Live Countdown Timer

The Countdown Timer tab shows a real-time, second-by-second countdown to any future date you enter. Simply type in the date and optionally give the event a name — New Year's Eve, birthday, vacation, project deadline, or exam date. The countdown updates every second and shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining. Perfect for motivation and planning.

Scientific Calculator

The integrated Scientific Calculator goes beyond basic arithmetic. It supports trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan and their inverses), logarithms (log base 10 and natural log), exponentials, square roots, cube roots, powers, modulo, absolute value, floor/ceiling/rounding, and the mathematical constants π and e. Toggle between basic and scientific mode using the button in the display.

Who Uses a Date Calculator?

  • Lawyers & Legal Teams — calculating statute of limitations, filing deadlines, and contract terms in business days.
  • Finance Professionals — settlement dates (T+2), bond maturity, loan terms, payment cycles.
  • Project Managers — sprint lengths, milestone dates, delivery schedules.
  • Students — assignment deadlines, exam countdowns, study schedules.
  • HR & Payroll — probation periods, notice periods, leave calculations.
  • Travelers — visa duration, passport validity, trip planning.
  • Healthcare — medication schedules, treatment durations, due date calculations.

Date Difference Formula

The number of days between two dates is calculated as: Days = (Date2 − Date1) / 86,400,000 ms. Business days are computed by iterating through each calendar day and counting only Monday through Friday. For months and years, the tool uses the Gregorian calendar's variable month lengths.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Date Calculator

How do I calculate the number of days between two dates online for free? ▾
Use FinanceKit Pro's free Date Calculator: enter your start date and end date in the "Date Difference" tab, then click Calculate. You'll instantly see total days, weeks, months, business days, and weekends — no sign-up needed.
How can I add days to a date and find the resulting date? ▾
Switch to the "Add / Subtract Days" tab. Enter your start date, the number of days you want to add (or a negative number to subtract), choose your unit (days/weeks/months/years), and optionally skip weekends. Click Calculate to see the resulting date and its weekday name.
What is a business day calculator and why does it matter? ▾
A business day calculator counts only Monday–Friday working days, excluding weekends. This is critical for legal deadlines, financial settlement dates (e.g., T+2), contract deliverables, and HR notice periods where weekends don't count. FinanceKit Pro shows both calendar days and business days side by side.
How many days until New Year 2027? ▾
Click the "New Year 2027" preset button in the Countdown tab and the live countdown will show you the exact number of days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining, updated every second in real time.
How do I find what day of the week a specific date falls on? ▾
Enter any date in the Date Difference or Add/Subtract calculator. The result shows the full weekday name (e.g., "Thursday, April 30, 2026"). This works for any past or future date across centuries.
How many working days are in 2026? ▾
In 2026, there are 365 calendar days. Of those, 261 are weekdays (Monday–Friday) and 104 are weekend days. After subtracting major public holidays (which vary by country), most countries have approximately 250–255 working days in 2026.
Can I use the scientific calculator for trigonometry and logarithms? ▾
Yes. Click the Scientific Calc tab and toggle to "SCIENTIFIC MODE" using the button in the display. You'll get sin, cos, tan, their inverses, log (base 10), natural log (ln), eˣ, powers, square roots, cube roots, absolute value, floor, ceil, round, and the constants π and e.
Is this date calculator free with no sign-up? ▾
Yes — 100% free with no account, no email, and no personal data collected. All calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. FinanceKit Pro works in all countries, all time zones, and all date formats.
