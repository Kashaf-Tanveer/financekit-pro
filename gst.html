What is GST and How Does It Work?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a broad-based consumption tax levied on most goods and services sold for domestic consumption. It is collected at each stage of the supply chain but ultimately borne by the end consumer. Different countries call it by different names: GST in Australia, Canada, India, and New Zealand; VAT (Value Added Tax) in the UK, Europe, and UAE; HST (Harmonized Sales Tax) in certain Canadian provinces. Despite the different names, the calculation method is the same.

GST Formulas Used in This Calculator

Adding GST:   GST Amount = Net Price × (Rate ÷ 100)
               Total Price = Net Price + GST Amount

Removing GST: Original Price = Total Price ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)
                GST Amount = Total Price − Original Price

Frequently Asked Questions — GST Calculator

How do I calculate GST in Australia 2026? +
Australia's GST rate is 10%. To add GST: multiply the price by 1.10. To remove GST from a GST-inclusive price: divide by 1.10. Example: a product costs $100 ex-GST → total is $110 including GST. Our free GST calculator Australia tab above does this instantly.
What is the difference between GST inclusive and GST exclusive prices? +
GST exclusive means the price does not include tax — GST will be added on top. GST inclusive (or GST included) means the price already contains the tax. For $100 ex-GST at 10%, the GST-inclusive price is $110. Our calculator handles both modes — select "Add GST" or "Remove GST" with the mode toggle.
How do I remove GST from a total price to find the original price? +
To reverse-calculate GST (remove GST from a GST-inclusive amount), divide the total by (1 + GST rate). For 10% GST: original = total ÷ 1.10. For NZ 15%: divide by 1.15. Switch to "Remove GST" mode above, enter the total, and our calculator gives you the original price and exact GST component instantly.
What is the GST / HST rate in Canada in 2026? +
Canada's federal GST rate is 5%. However, most provinces charge additional provincial tax. Ontario HST = 13%, British Columbia = 12% (GST 5% + PST 7%), Alberta = 5% (no PST), Quebec = 14.975% (GST 5% + QST 9.975%), Nova Scotia HST = 15%. Select "Ontario HST" or "Canada GST" in the quick-select above to calculate correctly.
What are the GST slabs in India in 2026? +
India's GST system has four main slabs: 5% (essential goods like sugar, tea), 12% (processed food, business class tickets), 18% (most services, electronics, soaps), and 28% (luxury goods, cars, tobacco, aerated drinks). Some essential items like raw food and books are at 0%. You can select any rate in the custom field on our calculator.
How does UK VAT work and how is it different from GST? +
UK VAT (Value Added Tax) functions identically to GST — it's a consumption tax added at each stage of production. Standard UK VAT rate is 20% in 2026. Reduced rates: 5% for children's car seats and energy. Zero-rated: most food, books, children's clothing. UK businesses must register for VAT if turnover exceeds £90,000/year.
Can I use this GST calculator to create a business invoice? +
Yes! Switch to "Multi-Item" mode above. You can add multiple products or services, each with their own quantity and price. The tool automatically calculates per-item GST, net total, total GST payable, and the grand total — giving you an invoice-ready breakdown that you can copy or print.
What is an Input Tax Credit (ITC) and how does it relate to GST? +
Input Tax Credit (ITC) allows GST-registered businesses to claim a deduction for the GST they paid on business purchases. For example, if you bought equipment for $11,000 (including $1,000 GST), you can claim that $1,000 back against GST you collect from customers. ITC reduces the net GST payable to the tax authority.
