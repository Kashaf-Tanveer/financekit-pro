Free Income Tax Calculator 2026 — USA, UK, Canada & Australia Explained

Our free income tax calculator for 2026 helps individuals and freelancers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia instantly estimate how much income tax they owe. Using official government tax brackets for each country, the tool calculates your federal income tax, effective tax rate, marginal rate, and monthly take-home pay in seconds — no spreadsheets, no accountant needed for a quick estimate.

How to Use This Income Tax Calculator

Using the calculator is simple. Select your country (USA, UK, Canada, or Australia), enter your gross annual income, choose your filing status (for USA), and optionally add any pre-tax deductions such as 401(k) contributions or pension payments. Click Calculate My Tax and you instantly see:

  • Your total income tax for the 2025–2026 tax year
  • Your effective tax rate and marginal (top) tax rate
  • A breakdown of tax owed in each bracket
  • Your annual and monthly take-home pay after tax
  • FICA/NI/Medicare contributions (where applicable)

Income Tax Formula Used

All calculations use the progressive (marginal) tax system official for each country. Only income within each bracket is taxed at that bracket's rate. For example, a US taxpayer earning $100,000 (single) does not pay 22% on all $100,000 — only the portion in the 22% bracket is taxed at 22%. The formula is:

Tax = Σ (each bracket's taxable amount × bracket rate)

where taxable income = gross income − standard deduction − pre-tax deductions.

USA Federal Income Tax Brackets 2025–2026 (IRS)

The IRS publishes updated inflation-adjusted brackets each year. For 2025, the standard deduction for a single filer is $15,000. After applying your deduction, the seven federal brackets apply: 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, and 37%. Our calculator applies all these automatically based on your filing status. Note: state income tax varies by state and is not included in the federal calculation but can be entered as an additional deduction.

UK Income Tax Rates 2025–2026 (HMRC)

For the 2025–26 tax year, UK taxpayers receive a Personal Allowance of £12,570 (zero rate). Above this, the Basic Rate of 20% applies on income up to £50,270. Income between £50,271 and £125,140 is taxed at 40% (Higher Rate), and income above £125,140 at 45% (Additional Rate). Our calculator also includes Class 1 National Insurance contributions at 8% between the Primary Threshold (£12,570) and Upper Earnings Limit (£50,270), and 2% above.

Canadian Federal Tax 2025–2026 (CRA)

Canada's federal income tax uses five progressive brackets: 15% on the first $57,375, 20.5% up to $114,750, 26% up to $158,519, 29% up to $220,000, and 33% above $220,000. The basic personal amount (BPA) of $16,129 applies as a non-refundable tax credit. Provincial tax is additional and varies; our calculator covers federal tax only. Canada does not use filing statuses like the USA.

Australian Income Tax 2025–2026 (ATO)

Australia's ATO applies the following rates for residents in 2025–26: 0% on the first $18,200 (tax-free threshold), 19% up to $45,000, 32.5% up to $120,000, 37% up to $180,000, and 45% on income above $180,000. The Medicare Levy of 2% is added on top of income tax (with low-income thresholds applying). Our calculator includes Medicare Levy by default.

Who Uses This Tax Calculator?

This tool is used by employees who want to know their take-home pay before accepting a job offer, freelancers and self-employed individuals planning quarterly tax payments, expats moving between countries who want to compare their after-tax income in different nations, and investors and high earners optimising their tax planning strategy. It is also widely used by HR professionals, financial advisors, and students learning tax concepts.

Frequently Asked Questions — Income Tax Calculator 2026

How do I calculate my income tax in the USA for 2026? +
Enter your gross annual income, select USA and your filing status (Single, Married Jointly, etc.), and the calculator instantly applies the official 2025–26 IRS progressive tax brackets. It deducts the standard deduction ($15,000 for single, $30,000 for MFJ in 2025), calculates your federal tax, FICA contributions, effective tax rate, and your monthly take-home pay — all in one click.
What is the income tax rate in the UK for 2026? +
For 2025–26, UK income tax is: 0% on the first £12,570 (Personal Allowance), 20% on £12,571–£50,270 (Basic Rate), 40% on £50,271–£125,140 (Higher Rate), and 45% above £125,140 (Additional Rate). National Insurance Class 1 is 8% between £12,570–£50,270 and 2% above. Our UK tax calculator applies all these bands and includes NI contributions automatically.
What is the difference between effective tax rate and marginal tax rate? +
Your marginal tax rate is the rate applied to the last dollar of your income — the highest bracket you fall into. Your effective (average) tax rate is your total tax paid divided by your gross income. For example, a US single filer earning $80,000 has a marginal rate of 22% but an effective rate of around 14.5%, because most of their income is taxed at 10% and 12%. Our calculator shows both.
How much income tax do I pay in Canada on $100,000 CAD? +
On CAD $100,000 federal income (2025–26 brackets), you pay approximately $17,739 in federal tax, after applying the Basic Personal Amount credit. Rate breakdown: 15% on first $57,375 = $8,606 tax, and 20.5% on the remaining $42,625 = $8,738 tax. This is federal only — provincial tax varies by province. Enter $100,000 with Canada selected to see the full breakdown instantly.
Does this calculator include state tax, provincial tax, or council tax? +
State income taxes (USA) and provincial taxes (Canada) vary widely by jurisdiction and are not included in the federal calculation. However, you can enter these as additional deductions in the "Additional Deductions" field to get a more accurate take-home figure. UK council tax and Australia's state levies are not included. This tool calculates federal (national) income tax only.
What is the Australian income tax for a $120,000 salary in 2025–2026? +
For an Australian resident earning $120,000 in 2025–26, the income tax is approximately $31,717 (without Medicare Levy) or $34,117 including the 2% Medicare Levy. Breakdown: $0–$18,200 at 0%, $18,201–$45,000 at 19% ($5,092), $45,001–$120,000 at 32.5% ($24,375) + low income tax offset reduces this. Select Australia and enter $120,000 to see the complete calculation.
Is this tax calculator accurate for filing my actual tax return? +
This calculator provides an accurate estimate for planning purposes using official published tax brackets. However, your actual tax return may differ due to: additional credits and deductions you qualify for, alternative minimum tax (AMT), capital gains rates, dividend income, self-employment tax, or any tax-deferred accounts. Always consult a licensed CPA, tax advisor, or use official government portals (IRS, HMRC, CRA, ATO) for your actual filing.
Can I use this tool for German income tax (Einkommensteuer) 2026? +
Yes! Select Germany (🇩🇪) in the country tabs. Germany uses a unique "splitting method" for married couples and a progressive formula. The 2025 German income tax applies: 0% up to €11,604 (basic allowance), a progressive zone 14%–42% from €11,605–€277,825, and a 45% solidarity surcharge zone above €277,826. The Solidaritätszuschlag (soli) of 5.5% on income tax also applies for higher earners.
