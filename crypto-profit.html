What Is a Crypto Profit Loss Calculator?

A crypto P&L calculator (profit and loss calculator) is a tool that helps traders and investors determine the exact financial outcome of a cryptocurrency trade. It takes into account your purchase price, selling price, the number of coins you hold, and any exchange trading fees — and calculates your net profit, net loss, return on investment (ROI), and break-even price. It is an essential tool for anyone trading Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), or any other digital asset.

In Simple Mode, enter your buy price (what you paid per coin), your sell price or current price, the quantity of coins you bought or sold, and your exchange fees (usually 0.1% for Binance, 0.5% for Coinbase). The calculator instantly shows your net profit or loss, ROI percentage, and break-even price. In Multiple Lots Mode, add up to 5 separate purchase entries to calculate your average cost basis — perfect for DCA (dollar cost averaging) strategies.

Crypto Profit & Loss Formula Explained

Investment Cost = Buy Price × Quantity + Buy Fees
Sale Proceeds = Sell Price × Quantity − Sell Fees
Net Profit/Loss = Sale Proceeds − Investment Cost
ROI % = (Net Profit / Investment Cost) × 100
Break-even Price = Investment Cost / Quantity
📊 Real Example: Bitcoin Trade

You bought 0.5 BTC at $30,000 each (total investment: $15,000) with 0.1% Binance fee ($15). You sell at $50,000 each (total sale: $25,000) with 0.1% fee ($25). Net profit = $25,000 − $25 − $15,000 − $15 = $9,960 profit (66.4% ROI). Break-even price = $15,015 / 0.5 = $30,030.

Why Crypto Trading Fees Matter More Than You Think

Many beginner crypto investors focus only on the price difference and ignore trading fees. On a $10,000 trade with 0.5% fees on both buy and sell sides, you pay $100 in fees — which means you need at least 1% price gain just to break even. On large positions, fees can make the difference between profit and loss. Our calculator includes both buy-side and sell-side fees so you always see your true net profit.

How to Calculate Average Cost Basis (DCA)

If you practice Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) — buying crypto in multiple smaller purchases over time — your average buy price matters more than any single purchase price. Use our Multiple Lots mode to enter each purchase separately. The calculator automatically computes your weighted average buy price across all lots, then shows your total profit or loss when you sell at the current price. This is especially useful for long-term Bitcoin and Ethereum holders who buy regularly.

Frequently Asked Questions — Crypto Profit & Loss Calculator

How do I calculate crypto profit and loss accurately? +
To calculate crypto profit and loss accurately, use the formula: Net Profit = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity − Total Fees. Always include both buy-side and sell-side exchange fees — most exchanges charge between 0.1% and 0.5% per trade. Our free crypto profit calculator does this automatically. Enter your buy price, sell price, quantity, and fees and get your exact result including break-even price and ROI percentage.
What is a good ROI percentage for crypto investment in 2026? +
There is no single "good" ROI for crypto — it depends on your risk tolerance, investment horizon, and the specific asset. Bitcoin has historically averaged 100–200% annual returns during bull markets. For context, 10–20% annual ROI is considered excellent in traditional stock markets. In crypto, because of higher volatility and risk, many traders target 50–200% per year. Always remember: past performance does not guarantee future returns. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.
How do I calculate percentage gain on Bitcoin or any crypto? +
Percentage gain formula: % Gain = ((Sell Price − Buy Price) / Buy Price) × 100. Example: You bought Ethereum at $1,500 and it is now worth $3,000. Gain = ((3,000 − 1,500) / 1,500) × 100 = 100% gain. If you want the gain after fees, use the net profit figure instead. Our calculator shows both gross percentage gain and net percentage gain after all trading fees are deducted.
Does this calculator include trading fees from Binance, Coinbase, and Kraken? +
Yes. You can enter any fee percentage. Common exchange fees are: Binance = 0.1% (0.075% with BNB), Coinbase Pro = 0.4–0.6%, Kraken = 0.16–0.26%, Bybit = 0.1%, OKX = 0.08%. Enter your exact fee percentage for both the buy and sell sides. The calculator will deduct them from your profit to show your true net gain or loss. On large trades, these fees significantly impact your actual take-home profit.
What is break-even price in crypto trading and why does it matter? +
The break-even price is the price at which you would neither profit nor lose money after all fees. Formula: Break-even = (Buy Price × Quantity + Buy Fees) / Quantity. For example, if you bought 1 BTC at $30,000 with a 0.1% fee ($30), your break-even price is $30,030 — you need Bitcoin to be above this price before you make any profit at all. Knowing your break-even price helps you set realistic stop-loss and take-profit levels.
How do I calculate profit from DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) crypto strategy? +
Use our Multiple Lots mode to calculate DCA profits. Add each of your separate purchases — enter the buy price and quantity for each purchase date. The calculator automatically computes your weighted average buy price across all lots and shows your total profit or loss at your chosen sell price. For example: you bought 0.1 BTC at $20,000, 0.1 BTC at $25,000, and 0.1 BTC at $30,000. Your average cost is $25,000. If BTC is now $45,000, your profit across 0.3 BTC is approximately $6,000.
