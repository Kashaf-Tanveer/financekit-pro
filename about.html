FinanceKit Pro is built on a strict no-data-collection philosophy. We believe your financial information is private. Here's exactly what we do and don't do:

Every calculation — EMI, loan, BMI, compound interest — runs entirely in your browser JavaScript. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions About FinanceKit Pro

Is FinanceKit Pro completely free to use in 2026? +
Yes, FinanceKit Pro is 100% free with no hidden charges, no subscription, and no sign-up required. All 50+ tools work directly in your browser at zero cost. The site is supported by display advertising so all tools remain permanently free for users worldwide — from the USA, UK, Australia, and Canada to Pakistan, India, and the UAE.
Does FinanceKit Pro collect my financial data or require an account? +
No — FinanceKit Pro collects zero personal data. You never need to create an account, enter your email, or share any personal information. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your loan amounts, income figures, and health data never leave your device. We use Google Analytics for anonymous traffic statistics only.
Which countries and currencies does FinanceKit Pro support? +
FinanceKit Pro works in 150+ countries with 170+ currencies. This includes all major currencies: USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, PKR, INR, AED, SAR, SGD, NZD, and more. EMI and loan calculators adapt to local conventions. The currency selector at the top of every page lets you switch currency globally across all tools instantly.
How accurate are the live crypto, gold, and currency prices? +
Live crypto prices come from CoinGecko's free API, updated every 5 minutes. Gold, silver, and oil prices are fetched from multiple commodity APIs and refreshed every 60 seconds. Currency exchange rates use open.er-api.com, updated hourly. Fear & Greed Index data comes from Alternative.me, updated daily. All data sources are clearly cited on each page. During market hours, prices are accurate to within 1–5 minutes of global spot rates.
Can I use FinanceKit Pro on my mobile phone? +
Yes — all FinanceKit Pro tools are fully mobile-responsive and work on any smartphone, tablet, or desktop. The site is optimized for Core Web Vitals performance (fast load times under 2 seconds), works on slower mobile connections, and supports both iOS Safari and Android Chrome. No app download required — just visit financekit.tech from any browser.
What makes FinanceKit Pro better than other free calculator websites? +
FinanceKit Pro uniquely combines financial calculators, live market data, and health tools in one place — with zero sign-up and zero data collection. Unlike most calculator sites that show static, outdated prices, FinanceKit Pro shows real-time crypto prices, live gold and silver rates, live currency rates, and a live economic calendar. It supports 170+ currencies for a truly global experience, and every tool has full dark mode, mobile responsiveness, and detailed explanations with formulas.
