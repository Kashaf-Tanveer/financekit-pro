Complete Guide to EMI Calculation in Pakistan 2026

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed monthly payment you make to repay your loan. Every EMI payment covers both the interest charged for that month and a portion of the principal. In early months, more of your payment goes toward interest; later, more goes toward principal. This is called a reducing balance or diminishing balance loan structure.

EMI Formula — How It Works

The EMI is calculated using this formula:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^N ÷ ((1+r)^N − 1)

Where: P = Principal loan amount | r = Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) | N = Total number of monthly payments (Tenure in months)

EMI Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI and how is it calculated? +
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed payment amount you pay monthly to repay a loan. It is calculated using: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^N ÷ ((1+r)^N − 1), where P = principal amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and N = total number of monthly payments. Each EMI covers both the interest charged for that month and a reducing portion of the principal amount.
What happens if I miss an EMI payment in Pakistan? +
Missing an EMI in Pakistan has serious consequences: (1) Late payment penalties — banks typically charge 1-2% per month on overdue amounts. (2) Credit score damage — reported to SECP and Credit Information Bureau (CIB), affecting future loan eligibility. (3) After 3 consecutive missed payments, the loan may be classified as Non-Performing (NPL). (4) Legal recovery proceedings may begin. Always contact your bank before missing a payment.
Can I reduce my EMI amount during the loan tenure? +
Yes! You can reduce your EMI through: (1) Balance transfer — move your loan to a bank with lower interest rates. (2) Loan restructuring — negotiate new tenure or rate with your current bank. (3) Prepayment — making extra payments reduces principal faster, allowing you to request a revised lower EMI. (4) Rate revision — for floating rate loans (linked to KIBOR in Pakistan), EMI changes when the benchmark rate changes.
Which is better — shorter tenure or lower EMI? +
Shorter tenure = higher EMI but much less total interest paid. Longer tenure = lower EMI but significantly more interest over the life of the loan. General rule: keep total monthly EMI payments below 40-50% of your net monthly income. If you can afford higher EMIs comfortably, choose shorter tenure — it saves you lakhs in interest. Use the "Extra Payment" feature above to see the impact of paying even ₨5,000 more per month.
How does home loan EMI differ from personal loan EMI? +
The EMI formula is identical for both, but they differ in: Interest rates (home loans: 8-13%, personal loans: 13-25%), Maximum tenure (home loans: up to 25-30 years, personal loans: typically 1-5 years), Loan amounts (home loans: up to ₨5 crore+, personal loans: typically up to ₨50 lakh), and Collateral (home loans secured against property, personal loans usually unsecured). The same calculator above works for both — just switch the tab and adjust the values.
How to include processing fee in EMI calculation? +
Processing fees (typically 0.5-2% of loan amount) are deducted upfront. To calculate the effective EMI including fees: add the processing fee to your loan amount (since you receive less but pay EMI on the full amount). Example: ₨10 lakh loan with 1% processing fee = you receive ₨9.9 lakh but pay EMI on ₨10 lakh. This raises your effective interest rate above the stated rate. Always ask for the Annualized Percentage Rate (APR) which includes all fees for a true cost comparison.
