How to Use This Free Tip Calculator Online (2026 Guide)

Our free tip calculator makes it effortless to compute the correct gratuity on any restaurant bill, delivery order, or service — and split it perfectly among friends. Simply enter your bill total, choose a tip percentage (or drag the slider), set the number of people, and hit Calculate. The Tip Calculation Formula

Formula Used
Tip Amount = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)
Total Bill = Bill + Tip Amount
Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People
Example: $85 bill × 20% tip = $17.00 tip → $102.00 total → $51.00 per person (2 people)

Tips on Splitting Bills Fairly

When dining out in groups, the bill splitting calculator makes fairness easy. Divide the total equally for the simplest approach. However, if people ordered significantly different amounts, consider itemized splitting. Our calculator handles equal splits perfectly — for itemized splitting, calculate each person's share individually and use our tool to add the appropriate tip portion on top.

Should You Tip on Pre-Tax or Post-Tax?

Most etiquette guides recommend tipping on the pre-tax subtotal, as the tax portion is not related to the service quality. However, in practice, many people simply tip on the total for convenience — the difference on a typical restaurant bill is usually small (a few dollars). Our calculator lets you enter whichever amount you prefer — you have full control.

How much should I tip at a restaurant in the US in 2026? +
In the United States in 2026, the standard restaurant tip is 18–20% for good service, 15% for acceptable service, and 25%+ for exceptional service. How do I calculate 20% tip on a restaurant bill quickly? +
Quick mental math for 20% tip: divide the bill by 5. For example, $85 ÷ 5 = $17.00 tip → $102.00 total. For 15%: find 10% (move decimal one place left), then add half. $85 → 10% = $8.50 → 15% = $12.75. Our free tip calculator does all of this automatically — just enter the amount and select your percentage.
