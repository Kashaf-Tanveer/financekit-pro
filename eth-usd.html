Ethereum (ETH) is the world's second-largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization and the leading smart contract platform powering decentralized finance (DeFi), NFTs, Web3 applications, and institutional blockchain infrastructure.

What is Ethereum (ETH)?

Ethereum is an open-source, decentralized blockchain platform launched on July 30, 2015 by Vitalik Buterin and a team of co-founders. Unlike Bitcoin which is primarily a store of value, Ethereum functions as a programmable blockchain — a global computing network where developers can build and deploy smart contracts and decentralized applications (dApps) without any centralized control or downtime.

The native currency of the Ethereum network is Ether (ETH), which is used to pay for computation (called "gas fees"), transfer value, participate in DeFi protocols, stake in the proof-of-stake consensus mechanism, and as a trading asset on every major cryptocurrency exchange worldwide.

In September 2022, Ethereum completed its historic "Merge" — transitioning from an energy-intensive Proof-of-Work (PoW) consensus model to Proof-of-Stake (PoS), reducing its energy consumption by over 99.95%. This made ETH a deflationary asset during periods of high network usage, as EIP-1559 burns a portion of transaction fees permanently.

How is the Ethereum Price Determined?

The ETH/USD price is determined by continuous supply and demand on cryptocurrency exchanges globally. Major exchanges contributing to ETH price discovery include Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Bybit, and Bitfinex. The price shown on this page is a volume-weighted average across these exchanges, giving you the most accurate global market reference rate.

Key factors that influence the Ethereum price include:

  • Network activity: Higher gas fees and DeFi usage generally signal demand for ETH
  • ETF inflows: Spot Ethereum ETF approval and institutional flows significantly impact price
  • Bitcoin price movements: ETH often follows BTC directionally
  • Macro environment: Interest rates, USD strength, and risk sentiment affect all crypto assets
  • Ethereum upgrades: Protocol improvements (EIP proposals, Dencun, Pectra upgrades) create price catalysts
  • DeFi & NFT activity: Total value locked (TVL) in Ethereum DeFi protocols correlates with ETH demand
  • ETH burn rate: EIP-1559 burns ETH permanently, reducing supply during high-usage periods

ETH to USD Conversion Formula

Converting Ethereum to US dollars is straightforward using the live ETH/USD exchange rate:

USD Value = ETH Amount × Current ETH/USD Price

Example: 2.5 ETH × $2,500 = $6,250 USD

ETH from USD: ETH = USD Amount ÷ ETH/USD Price Example: $5,000 ÷ $2,500 = 2.0 ETH

While Bitcoin (BTC) remains the largest cryptocurrency by market cap, Ethereum holds the #2 position and has unique characteristics that often drive independent price movements.
