What Is a Time Zone?

A time zone is a region of the Earth that observes the same standard time. The world is divided into 24 primary time zones, each roughly 15° of longitude wide, based on offsets from UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). For example, New York is UTC−5 (EST) or UTC−4 (EDT during daylight saving), London is UTC+0 (GMT) or UTC+1 (BST in summer), and Dubai is UTC+4 year-round.

Daylight Saving Time (DST) Support

Daylight saving time complicates global scheduling because different countries transition on different dates. The USA transitions in March and November, the UK in late March and October, while countries like Japan, India, UAE, and Saudi Arabia do not observe DST at all. Our converter uses your browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API, which is always DST-aware for every city and date combination.

Key Time Zone Conversions for Global Users

  • EST to GMT: New York is 5 hours behind London in winter, 4 hours in summer.
  • PST to IST: Los Angeles is 13.5 hours behind India (IST = UTC+5:30).
  • Dubai to New York: Dubai (UTC+4) is 8–9 hours ahead of New York.
  • Tokyo to London: Tokyo (UTC+9) is 9–10 hours ahead of London.
  • Sydney to Los Angeles: Sydney is 17–19 hours ahead depending on DST.

Formula: How Time Zone Conversion Works

The conversion formula is: Target Time = Source Time + (Target UTC Offset − Source UTC Offset). For example, converting 3:00 PM EST (UTC−5) to IST (UTC+5:30): 3:00 PM + (5:30 − (−5)) = 3:00 PM + 10:30 = 1:30 AM IST (next day). Our tool does this automatically, including date rollovers and DST adjustments.

What are forex market opening hours in different time zones? +
The four major forex trading sessions are: Sydney (10 PM UTC), Tokyo (midnight UTC / 9 AM JST), London (8 AM UTC / GMT), and New York (1 PM UTC / 9 AM EST). How do I convert PST to IST (India Standard Time)? +
IST (India Standard Time) is UTC+5:30, while PST (Pacific Standard Time) is UTC−8. That makes IST exactly 13 hours and 30 minutes ahead of PST. During US daylight saving (PDT, UTC−7), IST is 12 hours 30 minutes ahead. For example, 9:00 AM PST = 10:30 PM IST on the same day.
What is UTC time and why is it used globally? +
UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the global standard time reference that replaced GMT for scientific and technical purposes. All other time zones are expressed as positive or negative offsets from UTC. It is used by airlines, internet servers, financial markets, and government systems worldwide because it never changes for daylight saving time, making it the universal anchor for global coordination.
