How to Use This Loan Calculator — A Complete Guide

This free loan calculator for Pakistan helps you plan any type of loan — home loan, car loan, business loan, or personal loan. Simply enter your loan amount, the annual interest rate offered by your bank, and the repayment tenure, and get instant results including your monthly EMI, total interest cost, and a full month-by-month amortization schedule.

Understanding Your Amortization Table

The amortization table shows exactly how each EMI payment is split between principal and interest. In the early months of your loan, a larger portion goes toward interest. As the loan matures, more of each payment reduces the principal. This is why making extra payments in the early months has the most impact — it reduces the principal faster and saves significant interest over time.

Loan Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

How is monthly loan payment calculated? +
Monthly payment uses the reducing balance EMI formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^N ÷ ((1+r)^N − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and N is the number of monthly installments. This ensures each payment covers both interest for that month and a portion of the principal.
How can I reduce my loan EMI? +
You can reduce EMI by: (1) Increasing loan tenure — more months means lower per-payment amount. (2) Negotiating a lower interest rate or switching to a bank with better rates. (3) Making a larger down payment to reduce the principal. (4) Refinancing when market interest rates drop. Note that longer tenure means significantly more total interest paid — use this calculator to see the exact difference.
Flat rate vs reducing balance — what's the difference? +
Flat rate interest is calculated on the original principal throughout the tenure — making the effective rate much higher than stated. Reducing balance (diminishing balance) interest is calculated only on the outstanding balance each month. This calculator uses the reducing balance method, which is the standard used by all reputable banks in Pakistan. If a lender quotes a "flat rate," the actual effective rate is roughly double — always ask for the reducing balance equivalent.
Is it better to pay more down payment or choose shorter tenure? +
Both strategies reduce total interest, but they work differently: A larger down payment reduces the principal immediately — good if you have the cash. A shorter tenure keeps the same principal but forces faster repayment — good if you have consistent high income. If you can choose only one: a larger down payment is usually better as it reduces the interest-bearing base from day one. Combine both for maximum savings.
How does prepayment affect my loan? +
Prepayment reduces the outstanding principal, which reduces future interest charges. You can either shorten the tenure (pay off faster) or reduce the EMI amount. Most Pakistani banks allow partial prepayment, though some charge a penalty of 1-3% on the prepaid amount. Always check your loan agreement.
