Frequently Asked Questions — Loan Calculator

How is a monthly loan payment calculated? +
Monthly payment is calculated using the reducing balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^N ÷ ((1+r)^N − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and N is the number of monthly installments. This ensures each payment covers both interest and a portion of the principal, with interest reducing each month.
How can I reduce my loan monthly payment? +
You can reduce your monthly payment by: (1) Increasing the loan tenure — spreads payments over more months; (2) Negotiating a lower interest rate with your lender; (3) Making a larger down payment to reduce the principal; (4) Refinancing your loan when interest rates drop. Note that a longer tenure means more total interest paid over the life of the loan.
What is the difference between flat rate and reducing balance interest? +
Flat rate interest is calculated on the original principal throughout the entire loan tenure — making the effective interest rate much higher than stated. Reducing balance interest is calculated only on the outstanding principal each month, which decreases as you repay. This calculator uses the reducing balance method — the standard used by most banks worldwide. Always confirm which method your lender uses.
Is it better to pay a larger down payment or choose a shorter tenure? +
Both strategies reduce total interest paid. A larger down payment reduces the principal immediately, lowering both monthly payments and total interest. A shorter tenure increases your monthly payment but significantly reduces total interest over the loan's life. The best strategy depends on your cash flow situation — if you have liquidity, a larger down payment is typically better. If you prefer lower monthly commitments, extend tenure but try to make prepayments when possible.
How does loan prepayment affect my total interest? +
Prepayment (paying more than your regular monthly payment) reduces the outstanding principal faster, which in turn reduces the interest charged in subsequent months. You can either reduce your remaining tenure (pay off sooner) or reduce your monthly payment amount. Most lenders allow partial prepayments, though some charge a prepayment penalty. Always check your loan agreement before making extra payments.
